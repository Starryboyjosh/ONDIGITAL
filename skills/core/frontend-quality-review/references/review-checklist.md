# Frontend Review Checklist

## Render

- Page/app loads without console-breaking errors.
- Primary content is visible above the fold.
- Images, fonts, icons, videos, and canvases render.
- No accidental horizontal scroll.

## Responsive

- Check narrow mobile, common mobile, laptop, and wide desktop when practical.
- Navigation remains usable.
- Text wraps cleanly and long words cannot escape containers.
- Fixed toolbars and sticky elements do not cover content.

## Interaction

- Buttons, links, menus, tabs, inputs, sliders, and toggles have hover/focus/active/disabled states.
- Keyboard tab order is coherent.
- Forms validate and show save/submission states.
- Async actions show progress and failure.

## Visual Quality

- The primary action is visually clear.
- Related elements align and group consistently.
- Spacing scale is intentional.
- The palette is not dominated by one hue without a reason.
- Repeated cards, shadows, borders, and dividers each have a job.

## Accessibility

- Controls have accessible names.
- Contrast is adequate.
- Icon-only controls have labels or tooltips.
- Motion respects reduced-motion settings when significant.
